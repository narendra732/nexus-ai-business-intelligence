Q3 SALES PERFORMANCE REPORT
Company: Northstar Retail
Period: July to September 2026

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Total revenue for Q3 was $480,000, compared with $520,000 in Q2. Revenue declined by approximately 7.7 percent.

REGIONAL PERFORMANCE
The North region generated $190,000 in revenue.
The South region generated $150,000 in revenue.
The East region generated $90,000 in revenue.
The West region generated $50,000 in revenue.

SALES CHALLENGES
The West region experienced lower customer traffic.
The East region experienced inventory shortages affecting product availability.
The company also reported an increase in customer acquisition costs.

RECOMMENDATIONS
Improve inventory planning in the East region.
Investigate customer traffic trends in the West region.
Review marketing campaign efficiency to control acquisition costs.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION
This report does not provide exact customer counts, profit margins, or detailed monthly revenue figures.
